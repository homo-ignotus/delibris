---
title: A Road in October
order: 2
---
The road went on beyond the town,  
past hedges stitched with early rain;  
the fields were turning russet-brown,  
and summer would not come again.

We walked until the light grew thin,  
not knowing where the path might bend;  
and kept the changing landscape in  
a pocket full of afternoon.
