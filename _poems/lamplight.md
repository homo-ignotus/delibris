---
title: Lamplight
order: 1
---
The page is warm beneath the light,  
and rain withdraws beyond the pane;  
a sentence waits to be made right,  
then finds its way to sleep again.

The evening gathers round the chair,  
the embers take their time to glow;  
and all the words suspended there  
are words the quiet seems to know.
