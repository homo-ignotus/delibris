A house of endless halls, changing tides and silent statues becomes a universe of its own in this dreamlike novel.

What I liked most was the patient way in which the book asks us to inhabit Piranesi's attention before rushing to explain the mystery. Every observation feels like a form of devotion.

## The atmosphere

Few fictional places have stayed with me so vividly. Clarke creates a world that seems at once impossible and entirely self-consistent.

## Final thought

Mysterious, generous and beautiful: a book I wanted to begin again as soon as it ended.
