Some novels are remarkable for the large events they describe; this one is remarkable for how much it finds in an ordinary conversation.

The shifting relationship between Connell and Marianne is portrayed with extraordinary attention to power, uncertainty, class and the things people cannot quite say to one another.

## The writing

Rooney's spare prose creates room for the reader to notice small changes of feeling. A moment's hesitation can matter more than an entire chapter of dialogue.

## Final thought

A clear-eyed and quietly devastating book about being known—and the difficulty of allowing yourself to be known.
