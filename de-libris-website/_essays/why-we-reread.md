---
title: Why We Reread
order: 2
---
Returning to a familiar book is not quite the same as repeating an experience. The pages may be unchanged, but the reader has moved on.

A sentence that once seemed unimportant can become the centre of a chapter. A character we admired may strike us differently. Memory, too, edits what we expect to find.

Perhaps rereading is less a journey backwards than a way of measuring the distance we have travelled.
