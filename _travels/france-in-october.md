I arrived with a notebook, a few plans and the vague ambition to spend more time looking around than looking at a screen.

## The first morning

The streets were still quiet when I went out for coffee. I took the long way back, letting small details become the day's itinerary: a bookshop window, a particular shade of blue on a door, the smell of bread from an open bakery.

## An afternoon wandering

Some of the best moments of a trip are the ones for which you have no photograph. I sat in a little square for much longer than I had intended, content to watch people cross from one patch of sunlight to another.

## What I brought home

A few postcards, a rather heavy book, and the pleasant sense that the trip had lasted longer than the calendar suggested.
