A novel constructed around a wonderfully simple question: what would happen if we could try the lives we never lived?

Nora Seed finds herself in a library between life and death, where each volume contains a different version of her own story. The premise makes room for grief, possibility and the peculiar burden of imagining every road not taken.

## What stayed with me

The library itself is an irresistible image. Its shelves suggest both the abundance of possibility and the strange limits of our understanding of happiness.

## A reservation

At times the lesson is explained more directly than I would prefer. Even so, there is generosity in the book's underlying question.

## In brief

An accessible and affecting novel about regret, imperfect lives and finding value in the one we actually inhabit.
