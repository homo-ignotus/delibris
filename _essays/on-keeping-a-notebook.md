There is something reassuring about a notebook that asks nothing of you. Unlike a blank document on a screen, it does not offer a notification, a recommendation or the possibility of three dozen other tabs.

A notebook is also a place in which incompleteness is allowed. A half-thought can remain a half-thought for months. An observation made on a train need not explain itself immediately.

## Small beginnings

Perhaps the most useful thing about keeping a notebook is learning to pay attention. The habit encourages one to notice the things that might otherwise pass unrecorded.
